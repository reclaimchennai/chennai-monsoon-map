Profile
     Royapuram zone lies in Latitude 13.10465 °, Longitude 80.27447 ° covering an area
of 42.33 Sq.km.

      There are 1,427 roads, out of which 1,370 roads to a length of 220.38 km are Interior
Roads, 57 Roads to a length of 37.98 Km are Bus Route Roads which are maintained by
GCC.
